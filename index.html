A LITTLE ABOUT US

We’re not trying to
have it all figured out.

I’m usually the one asking what the point is, whether it’s worth the money, whether we’re actually moving forward, and why we’ve agreed to do something that sounded better yesterday.

I’m usually the one noticing the light changed, the room got quieter, the coffee was better than expected, or that we’ve been walking for twenty minutes without saying anything.

We make plans. We change them. We disagree about very small things. We go somewhere anyway. We buy the occasional thing we absolutely did not need. Then one of us usually admits the other one was right.

I’M CARA
